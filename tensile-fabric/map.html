How to read this map

The mesh is society: every line is a tie between neighbors, and it behaves like surface tension on a droplet — load spreads through the weave until one thread exceeds its bond energy. Pressing makes a gravity well: a shock, a grievance, a crisis pulling on everyone near it.

Line color is strain: steel is slack, gold is loaded, rust is near failure. When a tie snaps it dumps its load onto its neighbors — stress concentrates at the crack tip, so tears propagate (Granovetter's cascade, Eq. 8 of the white paper).

Bond energy per tie is w = shadow-of-future × salience (Eq. 10). Slide salience p to 1 and the fabric goes slack without any load at all — perfect predictability is anomie, the second failure mode. Slide δf down and every knot weakens at once. Heterogeneity σ widens the weak tail where cracks start.

Polarize cuts the bridge ties down the middle: small f, catastrophic loss of S. Random decay removes the same ties diffusely: the fabric barely notices until f nears the fracture point f_c = 1 − 1/⟨k⟩ (gold tick).
